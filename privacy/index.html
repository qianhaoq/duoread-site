DuoRead Privacy Policy

简体中文

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 22 日。

DuoRead 用于本地阅读与笔记，不收集任何数据。DuoRead 无账号系统，不提供云同步，App 不发起任何网络请求。

导入的文档副本、笔记、文本与 PDF 阅读进度保存在应用本地存储空间。App 不将这些数据发送给开发者或第三方，也不出售或共享这些数据。根据你的系统设置，这些数据可能包含在你自己的 iCloud 或设备备份中。你主动导出的笔记会保存到你在系统文件导出器中选择的位置，包括 iCloud Drive 或第三方云文件提供方；该位置的数据处理遵循相应服务的政策。

应用通过系统文件选择器访问你主动选择的 Markdown、PDF 或 txt 文件，用于导入和阅读，并将副本保存到应用本地空间。应用不需要访问与你选择的文件无关的内容。若你选择第三方文件提供方，其自身的数据处理遵循该提供方的政策；DuoRead 不提供该服务的同步功能，也不因此发起网络请求。

DuoRead 无追踪、分析或广告，不集成任何第三方 SDK。

你可以在文档列表中删除导入的文档及其关联笔记和阅读进度，不影响导入来源的原文件。删除 App 可移除其本地保存的数据。请自行保管原文件；系统备份和导出副本由你及相应服务管理，删除应用内数据不会删除这些副本。DuoRead 不提供应用内跨设备同步。

English

Effective date: October 22, 2026.

DuoRead is a local reading and note-taking app and does not collect any data. DuoRead has no account system or cloud sync. The app does not initiate any network requests.

Imported document copies, notes, and text and PDF reading progress are stored locally in app storage. The app does not send this data to the developer or third parties, or sell or share it. Depending on your system settings, this data may be included in your own iCloud or device backups. Notes you explicitly export are saved wherever you choose in the system file exporter, including iCloud Drive or third-party cloud file providers; data at that location is handled under the relevant service’s policy.

The app uses the system file picker to access Markdown, PDF, or plain text files you select for importing and reading. It saves a copy in local app storage and does not need access to unrelated files. If you choose a third-party file provider, that provider handles data under its own policy; DuoRead does not provide synchronization with that service or initiate network requests as a result.

DuoRead has no tracking, analytics, or advertising and includes no third-party SDKs.

You can delete imported documents and their associated notes and reading progress from the document list without changing the original source files. Deleting the app removes its locally stored data. Keep your original files safe; system backups and exported copies are managed by you and the relevant services, and deleting in-app data does not delete those copies. DuoRead does not provide in-app cross-device sync.
